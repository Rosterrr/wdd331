NP Membership Benefits

This level is designed for non profit organizations and has no fee.

  • Community networking opportunities
  • Access to chamber announcements
  • Participation in selected community events

Bronze Membership Benefits

This level provides basic support for local businesses.

  • Business listing in the chamber directory
  • Access to member events
  • Basic networking opportunities

Silver Membership Benefits

This level offers more visibility and professional development opportunities.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Training workshops
  • Advertising opportunities
  • Discounts for selected chamber events

Gold Membership Benefits

This level provides the highest amount of exposure and chamber support.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Homepage spotlight opportunities
  • Premium event discounts
  • Priority promotion in chamber activities
