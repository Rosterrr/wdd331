Explore El Salvador Site Plan

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Explore El Salvador

This name was selected because the website will help visitors discover travel destinations, culture, food, beaches, volcanoes, and historical places in El Salvador. The name is simple, memorable, and clearly connected to tourism.

Optional domain: exploreelsalvador.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide helpful tourism information about El Salvador. The site will highlight popular destinations, local food, cultural experiences, travel tips, and recommended places to visit. It will help tourists and local visitors plan trips and learn more about the country.

Scenarios

  • What are the best places to visit in El Salvador for a first-time tourist?
  • Where can I find information about beaches, volcanoes, food, and cultural destinations?
  • What activities can visitors do while traveling in El Salvador?

Color Scheme

The website will use colors inspired by El Salvador’s flag, nature, and tourism identity.

Primary Blue
#0057A8
Accent Yellow
#F9C74F

Primary Blue: Used for headings, header, footer, navigation, and important design accents.

Accent Yellow: Used for buttons, highlights, borders, and call-to-action areas.

Light Background: Used behind content sections to keep the page clean and easy to read.

Typography

Georgia: Used for main headings and section headings to give the site a formal and attractive look.

Arial: Used for body text, paragraphs, navigation, and general page content because it is clean and easy to read.
